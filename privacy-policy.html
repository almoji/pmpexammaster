Privacy Policy

PMP Exam Master respects your privacy. This application stores only the information required to provide your study experience.

Data Stored Locally

  • Practice history
  • Mock exam results
  • Dashboard statistics
  • Study progress
  • Application preferences

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal information
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Payment information

Data Sharing

All study information is stored locally on your device. PMP Exam Master does not collect, sell, transmit or share your personal information with third parties.

Third-Party Services

PMP Exam Master does not use advertising services and does not share your study data with third parties.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will be published on this page.
